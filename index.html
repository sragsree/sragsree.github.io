The tradeoff behind the approach

Recurring remediation was consuming engineering time. I chose a focused path to reach operations sooner and made operational ownership part of the plan.

Alternative

Broader platform integration

A longer path to the immediate repair capability.

The choice

Automation in the repair platform

Earlier delivery, with a framework operators could extend.

My contribution
Technical direction, team formation, and operational handoff, supported by an initial Java/Spring prototype remotely invoking Python remediation scripts. The team carried the framework into production.
Another decision: make failure patterns usable
I established structured error signatures as a shared input and aligned customer teams on providing them. Combined with AI-assisted analysis, this helped engineers find recurring patterns and shorten investigation.
